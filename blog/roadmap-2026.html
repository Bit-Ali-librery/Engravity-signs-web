2026 Roadmap: what's coming next in Engravity

When we launched Engravity v1.0 in March, we promised to build this tool with the community. That means full transparency about what we're working on, what we dropped, and why. This is our real 2026 roadmap — not a feature list invented to sell.

We've split it by quarter. Priorities can change (and they will) based on what we hear from you.

Q2 2026 — Solid foundations

Student plan BEING DEFINED

The free plan for verified students. We're still deciding whether to verify by .edu email or by manual review. If you have an opinion or feedback, write to us.

Vector engine improvements IN DEVELOPMENT

Pen tool with live preview, smart snapping between paths, and a new node-edit mode that cuts the number of clicks for common tasks. Closed beta starts this month.

CorelDRAW import (.cdr) SHIPPED

We worked on this for months. We can finally import .cdr files while preserving layers, gradients, and editable text.

Q3 2026 — Production and precision

CNC simulation with real materials

The 3D simulation we already have is moving from "pretty render" to "predictive render": it'll show how an actual cut looks on cast vs. extruded acrylic, how the wood grain behaves, and where tear-out is likely before it happens.

Custom machine profiles

Today we ship 6 preconfigured industrial machines. We'll let any user on the Industrial or Studio plan build their own profile with their own parameters, spindles, and post-processor.

Per-project cost reports

Calculating what it costs to produce a sign (material + machine time + finishing) is still manual for almost everyone. We're going to bake it into Engravity, with templates you can adapt to your own price list.

Q4 2026 — Teamwork

Shared libraries

For studios and shops with multiple designers: a library of elements, materials, and templates that lives in the cloud and syncs across teams. Versioned and permissioned.

In-canvas comments and reviews

Today, client revisions bounce around in WhatsApp and email. We want them to live inside the file: pin a point, leave a comment, see the history.

Public API v1

To integrate Engravity with your ERP, quoting system, or existing production flow. REST endpoints, webhooks, and token authentication.

What we decided NOT to do in 2026

Saying what's coming is only half the story. Saying what isn't matters just as much. These are features many people asked for that we decided not to build this year:

  • Paid template marketplace. We think it's important to keep the collaborative spirit. Community-built templates will stay free for Student and Professional users.
  • Full mobile app. Designing signs on a 6-inch screen is frustrating for everyone. We're going to ship a mobile viewer for review and approval, but not an editor.
  • Lithographic print support. Not our world. There are better tools for that, and we want to stay focused on CNC and three-dimensional production.

How to influence this roadmap

The roadmap gets updated every quarter. If a feature matters to you and you don't see it here, tell us. We set priorities by listening to people who already use Engravity day to day. There's no product committee in a far-away room.

Write to us at hola@engravitysigns.com or open an issue on GitHub. We read everything — even when it takes us a while to reply.
