2026 Roadmap: what's coming next in Engravity

When we launched Engravity v1.0 in March, we promised to build this tool together with the community. That means total transparency about what we're working on, what we dropped, and why. This is our real 2026 roadmap — not a feature list invented to sell.

We split it by quarter. Priorities can change (and they will) based on what we hear from you.

Q2 2026 — Solid foundations

Student plan IN DEFINITION

The free plan for verified students. We're deciding whether it's by .edu email or by manual validation. If you have an opinion or feedback, write to us.

Vector engine improvements IN DEVELOPMENT

Pen tool with live preview, smart snapping between paths, and a new node-edit mode that reduces the number of clicks for common tasks. Closed beta starts this month.

CorelDRAW import (.cdr) SHIPPED

We've been working on this for months. Finally importing .cdr files preserving layers, gradients, and editable text.

Q3 2026 — Production and precision

CNC simulation with real materials

The 3D simulation that already exists is going from "pretty render" to "predictive render": it'll show how the actual cut looks on cast vs. extruded acrylic, how the wood grain behaves, and where tear-out might appear before it happens.

Custom machine profiles

Today we support 6 preconfigured industrial machines. We'll let any user on the Industrial or Studio plan create their own profile with their parameters, spindles, and post-processor.

Per-project cost reports

Calculating how much it costs to produce a sign (material + machine time + finishing) is still manual for almost everyone. We're going to integrate it inside Engravity, with templates that adapt to your price list.

Q4 2026 — Teamwork

Shared libraries

For studios and shops with multiple designers: a library of elements, materials, and templates that lives in the cloud and syncs across teams. Versioned, with permissions.

Comments and reviews on the canvas

Today client revisions travel by WhatsApp and email. We want them to live inside the file: pin a point, leave a comment, see the history.

Public API v1

To integrate Engravity with your ERP, quoting system, or existing production flow. REST endpoints, webhooks, token authentication.

What we decided NOT to do in 2026

As important as saying what's coming is saying what isn't. These are features many people asked us for that we decided not to build this year:

  • Paid template marketplace. We think it's important to keep the collaborative spirit. Templates the community creates will be shared free in the Student and Professional plans.
  • Full mobile app. Designing signs on a 6-inch screen is frustrating for everyone. We're going to make a mobile viewer for review and approval, but not an editor.
  • Lithographic print support. Not our world. If you need that, there are better tools and we want to focus on CNC and three-dimensional production.

How to influence this roadmap

The roadmap gets updated every quarter. If a feature is important to you and you don't see it here, tell us. Priority decisions are made by listening to people already using Engravity day to day. There's no product committee in a far-away room.

Write to us at hola@engravitysigns.com or open an issue on GitHub. We read everything — even if it sometimes takes us a while to reply.
