Frequently asked questions

Is the Student plan really free?

Yes. We're still finalizing the model, but the idea is that any student or maker can use Engravity to learn without barriers. It's our way of giving back to the community that shaped us.

Can I change plans at any time?

Yes. Upgrade or downgrade whenever you need to and we'll only charge the prorated difference. No long contracts, no penalties.

Does Engravity replace Illustrator or CorelDRAW?

No. Engravity complements them. Import your AI, CDR, or DXF files and add what those programs don't have: ADA validation, 3D material simulation, and direct CNC production. Where your design ends, Engravity begins.

Which CNC machines are supported?

The Industrial plan includes 6 industrial profiles (Mach3, GRBL, LinuxCNC, Mastercam, and two proprietary formats). The Studio plan lets you add custom profiles. If you work with a specific machine, write to us and we'll validate it with you.

Is there a trial period?

Yes. The Professional and Industrial plans include a 14-day trial with no credit card required. When it ends, you can switch to the free Student plan if you need more time to decide.

How does the annual discount work?

Paying annually saves you 20% over the monthly price. We charge once a year and you can cancel with a prorated refund within the first 30 days.

Do my files stay in Engravity if I stop paying?

Your files are always yours. Even if you cancel your subscription, you keep read-only access to your projects for 12 months so you can export them in standard formats (AI, DXF, SVG).
